Recap

01
The workshop involved 15 participants across GBS, FSQ, IT/Tech, Consumer Advocacy & Marketing Transformation, covering the full E2E GCEM across 5 layers — Consumer, Contact Center (L1), Escalation (L2 & L3), FSQ, and Reporting & Analytics — across 4 channels (Phone, Email, Social, Physical Mail) in EU (UKI + Benelux) and NA.
02
Pre-workshop, the team ran 9 weeks of structured preparation across 20+ virtual sessions — SIPOC workshops for NA & EU, Swimlane mapping, AS-IS process mapping, and Gemba walks with 9 cross-functional teams across both regions.
03
Pre-work uncovered 36 pain points and the workshop identified 33 opportunities, all working toward producing ONE cross-functionally aligned, future-state Global Operating Model.

Outcomes

01
Define the future ways of working for how Kraft Heinz handles consumer engagement globally — with an intent to fully transform into Contact Centre of the Future by bringing together Omnichannel Environment, Voice of Consumer, AI Enablement and Intelligent Engagement into one connected model.
02
All 33 opportunities classified by impact & effort, across 5 Projects — Sonic, NA Tech Transformation, Continuous Improvement, Consumer Pulse & ROW Implementation.
03
Project Sonic opportunities identified for fast, visible impact; delivering near-term wins in parallel with longer-term transformation.
04
The To-Be state introduces these key capabilities: Reason Hierarchy Code Standardization, Revamped Knowledge Base, Omnichannel Environment, Global Escalation Matrix, Language Translation & AI Consumer Assist, Salesforce Auto Transcription, Global VOC Reporting & Governance Model.
